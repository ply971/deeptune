# R2: abstention ("not sure") with a target error

Branch `video/R2-abstention`, on top of `video-analysis` 8512a09. Everything here ran on the CPU, on synthetic scores or tiny clips with `dummy_video3d`. **No number in this report describes a real model on real data.** No feature here promises a fixed accuracy.

## The guarantee, exactly

There are two, chosen with `--abstain_guarantee`. In both, the unit is the group. Both are conditional on the fitted model, and calibration groups must be disjoint from train, validation and test.

**`selective_risk` (default): a bound on error among answered clips.**
- *Quantity.* For threshold t, draw a new group with at least one answered clip, then one of its answered clips uniformly. R(t) is the chance that this clip is wrong. It is group-weighted: each answered group counts once.
- *Statement.* P(R(t_hat) <= target) >= 1 - delta, over the draw of the calibration groups. This is a high-probability bound, not a marginal one. `delta` defaults to 0.05.
- *Assumption.* Calibration groups and new groups are i.i.d. from one population. Clips inside a group can depend on each other in any way.
- *Procedure.* Learn then Test (Angelopoulos, Bates, Candes, Jordan, Lei, arXiv 2110.01052; checked against the paper's text):
  - one null, R(t) > target, per threshold on a 32-point grid fixed before seeing data, even in log(1 - t);
  - the Hoeffding-Bentkus p-value (Prop. 1, Eq. 2) on per-group losses, which lie in [0, 1];
  - Bonferroni (Prop. 2), which gives family-wise error control and so risk control with no monotonicity needed (Thm. 1);
  - the lowest rejected threshold.
- *Conditioning.* The paper's selective-classification section (Sec. 3.2) divides by the number of answered points, and the text I read does not spell out the justification. Mine: given the number N(t) of answered groups, their losses are i.i.d. draws from the conditional law, so the p-value is valid given N(t), and so overall.
- *One clip per unit.* The loss is then a Bernoulli, so the exact binomial tail is used, with no factor e.

**`silent_error`: split conformal on one score per group.**
- *Quantity.* A group's score is the highest score among its wrong clips (minus infinity if none). The threshold sits just above the k-th smallest, k = ceil((n + 1)(1 - target)).
- *Statement.* P(the new group has any answered wrong clip) <= target, marginally over the calibration groups and the new group.
- *Assumption.* Exchangeable groups only.
- *What it does not say.* It does not bound error among answered clips: a rule answering 10% of clips can be wrong on half of them.

**Not guaranteed by either:** accuracy on any one clip, group or class; per-class error; clip-weighted error when big groups err more (`test_group_weighted_error_can_be_far_from_clip_weighted_when_big_groups_err` shows 0.1 against 0.5 by hand); new clips from a group already seen; any distribution shift; wrong calibration labels; other weights or scoring settings.

**Sources for group handling.** Dunn, Wasserman & Ramdas (JASA, arXiv 1809.07441) study conformal prediction sets for two-layer data, and that is the context. I did not implement their CDF pooling or subsampling. SGR (Geifman & El-Yaniv, arXiv 1705.08500) is the origin of risk-controlled rejection. I did not use its binary search, because its tests depend on the data path, whereas Bonferroni on a fixed grid needs no argument about that.

**Exchangeability in the actual split.** Calibration groups are a uniform draw from the training split, and that split came from `split_dataset`'s stratified grouped folds, as test did by another fold. So calibration groups and test groups are not exactly i.i.d. draws, and the test check is close to the theory rather than identical to it. The guarantee speaks about new groups from the population that calibration represents.

## Realised coverage and "loose" (item 4)

- `silent_error`: coverage given one calibration set is Beta(n + 1 - l, l), l = floor((n + 1) target) (Angelopoulos & Bates, arXiv 2107.07511, Sec. 3.2), with n = groups. Group scores with ties (several groups with no wrong clip) make true coverage larger, so `exact_beta_form` is false and only the lower end holds.
- `selective_risk`: P-118 warned to check the form, and that Beta form does **not** apply, since the threshold is picked from a grid rather than by rank. `realised_coverage` is instead the share of new groups with no answered wrong clip, as a Clopper-Pearson interval (Beta quantiles) on n groups, Bonferroni-corrected over the grid so it holds for the picked threshold.
- **Effective n:** groups for `silent_error`; answered groups for `selective_risk`, since only those carry evidence.
- **How the "loose" n was chosen.** Loose when the upper end of the 95% interval of the realised miss rate, Beta(l, n + 1 - l), is above 1.5 x target: one calibration draw can plausibly miss half as much again as the target says. It depends on the target because the same n is ample at 20% and hopeless at 1%. The factor 1.5 is a judgement call. The interval is always shown.

| target | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.05 | 0.02 | 0.01 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| loose below (groups) | 70 | 160 | 342 | 903 | 1810 |

By P-118's own estimate of about 182 calibration groups on UCF101, a 10% target would not be called loose, and a 5% target would. That is arithmetic on a stated group count, not a measurement.

| stress: whole groups err, no threshold can meet it | 60 | 16 | 0.116 | 0.000 | 0.00 | - | 0.151 | 0.15 | 0.91 |

How to read it:
- **Counting clips breaks in the stress case:** 15.1% false claims against a stated 10%, where counting groups gave none. In the milder dependent cases counting clips did not exceed 10%, because Bonferroni's margin absorbed the overstatement. It just answered more (0.58 against 0.40). So the violation is real but needs strong dependence near the boundary, and whether real video gets there is unmeasured.
- **The group rule is conservative.** Zero false claims here means slack, not perfection. On the dependent case, in a smaller exploratory run of 100 draws, picked thresholds had a true risk of about 0.057 against the 0.10 target. The bound leaves answers on the table, which is the cost of a guarantee with no model of the dependence.
- **The p-value itself is tight at the boundary.** A single threshold with exact binomial on i.i.d. clips lets false claims through close to delta, and `test_the_exact_p_value_is_valid_and_not_vacuous_at_the_boundary` asserts it is above half of delta and at most delta.

## Decisions

1. **Selective risk is the default**, since "target error" reads as error when it answers. It costs a delta and conservatism.
2. **Group-weighted, not clip-weighted.** A clip-weighted bound would need a known cap on group size, and with unbounded sizes no distribution-free test exists. The clip-weighted error is reported beside it.
3. **Deterministic per-group means over a random one-clip draw per group.** A draw would allow the exact binomial test without the factor e, but the output would depend on a seed.
4. **Grid of 32.** The zero-error floor is ceil(log(delta / 32) / log(1 - target)) answered groups: 62 at 10% with delta 0.05. A coarser grid helps slightly and a finer one costs little. Not tuned on data.
5. **The fit runs last in `deeptune.py`,** after test scoring and with no try/except, as B3's report does, so a fault costs no earlier output and is visible. The fit does not read test rows; the report that follows reads the test predictions and fits nothing.
6. **The calibration seed stays fixed under `--seed`,** like the split, so seeds share calibration groups.

## Open problems

- **Not done:** conformal prediction sets. Output is an answer or "not sure", never a set of classes. No numbered item asked for sets, but the task title mentions conformal prediction, so say if you want them.
- **Not done:** cross-conformal on out-of-fold scores, the alternative in P-114. Those scores come from other models than the shipped one, so its validity would need its own argument. B3's `oof_predictions.csv` has what it would need.
- **Classification only.** Regression is refused, since it has no error rate without a tolerance. Other modalities are refused too.
- **`--target_error` lowers training data,** by default about 1/7 of the training split. The effect on accuracy is unmeasured.
- **Fit on one target.** The fit table's `simultaneous_upper_bound` holds across thresholds, but re-picking a target after seeing test results is model selection on test. The README says so; nothing enforces it.
- **Choosing a `--abstain_guarantee` or score after seeing results** adds selection the bound does not cover.
- **No GUI yet.** Phase 7 should show `decision` and `headline`. The fields are in `abstention_summary.json`, and `evaluators.abstention` is torch-free.
- **`max_prob` near 1.** Scores above 1 - 1e-6 are not told apart by the grid, so an overconfident model may have few usable thresholds. A real-data check.
- **Label noise** is outside the guarantee. Real datasets have some.
- `README`/help promise nothing beyond this report; the coordinator's and ml-critic's reviews are still to come.
